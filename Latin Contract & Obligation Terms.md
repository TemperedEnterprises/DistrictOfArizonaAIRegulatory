# **Latin Contract & Obligation Terms**

These terms relate specifically to agreements, duties, and the formation
or breach of obligations.

-   **Caveat Actor:** \"Let the doer beware.\" Similar to *caveat
    > emptor*, applying the principle of caution to the person taking
    > the action.

-   **Caveat Emptor:** \"Buyer beware.\" The principle that the buyer
    > alone is responsible for checking the quality and suitability of
    > goods before a purchase is made.

-   **Consensus Ad Idem:** \"Agreement to the same thing.\" Essential
    > for the formation of a valid contract, meaning both parties
    > understand and agree to the same terms.

-   **Ex Gratia:** \"By favor.\" Something done voluntarily and out of
    > kindness, not based on legal obligation.

-   **Pacta Sunt Servanda:** \"Agreements must be kept.\" A fundamental
    > principle of contract law and international law.

-   **Uberrima Fides:** \"Utmost good faith.\" A doctrine applying to
    > certain contracts (like insurance) requiring full honesty and
    > disclosure from both parties.

# **Latin Evidence & Proof Terms**

Terms related to the presentation and nature of evidence and the burden
of proof in court.

-   **Prima Facie:** \"On the face of it\" or \"at first sight.\" Refers
    > to evidence that is sufficient to establish a fact or raise a
    > presumption unless disproved.

-   **Res Gestae:** \"Things done.\" Spontaneous statements made during
    > or immediately after an event, admissible as evidence as they are
    > considered part of the event itself.

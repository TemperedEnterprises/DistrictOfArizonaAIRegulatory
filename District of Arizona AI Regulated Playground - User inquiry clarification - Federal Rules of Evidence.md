3. **Control Information Flow:** Structured evidentiary presentation.  
4. **Protect Privilege and Confidentiality:** Attorney-client, spousal, and similar relationships.  
5. **Prevent Unfair Prejudice:** Exclusion of emotionally manipulative or confusing evidence.

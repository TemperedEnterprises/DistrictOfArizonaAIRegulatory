# I. Executive Summary

The Mediation and Assessment Program (MAP) is a judicially integrated,
digitally administered court system that expands the principles of the
Alternative Dispute Resolution Act to include technology-driven
adjudication under a specialized framework. The MAP architecture mirrors
Article III courts while functioning entirely online, with structured
roles and enhanced procedural access for pro se and disabled litigants.
This document outlines the structure, ethical mandates, technical
infrastructure, and jurisdiction of MAP.

# II. Purpose and Jurisdiction

MAP shall operate as a digital-first \"Small Claims\" court encompassing
not only traditional low-value claims (e.g., contract defaults, damage
assessments) but also technologically traceable harms and ADA compliance
cases under 42 U.S.C. § 12101 et seq. The system is built to enhance
equitable access, streamline conflict resolution, and reduce procedural
delays using Rapid Application Development (RAD) programming paradigms.

# III. Confidentiality and Judicial Regulation

All filings in MAP shall be public by default. Confidentiality may only
be conferred by explicit order to seal the case issued by a judge. This
ensures transparency, accountability, and public access to court
activities unless sealing is specifically warranted and ordered.

# IV. Court Organization and Appointments

The MAP system shall include:\
• Judicial Senior Judge\
• Senior Magistrate Judge\
• Court-appointed attorney for every pro se litigant\
• AI roles:\
- AI Ethics Specialist\
- AI Systems Engineer\
- AI Orchestrator\
- AI Systems Compliance Officer\
All roles are integrated into the recursive operational framework
ensuring functional legality, technological validation, and ethical
oversight.

# V. ADA Court Integration

A dedicated ADA online court shall be established under MAP to handle
all filings under the Americans with Disabilities Act. Subpoena and
disposal mechanisms shall be made available to the plaintiff immediately
upon case creation. These tools shall be regulated by the Director of
MAP, with transparency and audit logs embedded in all actions.

# VI. Technological and Data Integration

MAP will implement RAD (Rapid Application Development) methods to build
modular, scalable, and auditable workflows. All documents and filings
will be centralized, with logs feeding directly into the AI learning
architecture that upholds judicial ethics and logic traceability. MAP is
the first system to formally integrate Recursive Legal AI with
structural court operations.

# VII. Attorney Discipline and Public Service

or in the District of Arizona, the Arizona Bar shall immediately assign
no fewer than five (5) hours of community service, in addition to any
formal disciplinary measures imposed. This policy emphasizes restorative
justice and recommitment to public duty.

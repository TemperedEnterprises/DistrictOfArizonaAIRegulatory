**COMPLAINT**

Plaintiff, John Smith, by and through undersigned counsel, for his
Complaint against Defendant, SuperDoo Inc., states as follows:

**I. INTRODUCTION**

1.  This is an action for damages and other relief arising from
    > Defendant SuperDoo Inc.\'s failure to provide a transferable title
    > for a vehicle purchased by Plaintiff John Smith, rendering the
    > vehicle unsaleable and causing financial loss.

**II. JURISDICTION AND VENUE**

2.  This Court has subject matter jurisdiction over this action pursuant
    > to 28 U.S.C. § 1332 because there is complete diversity of
    > citizenship between the Plaintiff and the Defendant, and the
    > amount in controversy exceeds the sum or value of \$75,000,
    > exclusive of interest and costs.

3.  Plaintiff John Smith is a citizen of the State of \[State other than
    > where SuperDoo Inc. is incorporated or has its principal place of
    > business\].

4.  Defendant SuperDoo Inc. is a corporation incorporated under the laws
    > of the State of \[State different from Plaintiff\'s citizenship\]
    > with its principal place of business in \[State different from
    > Plaintiff\'s citizenship\].

5.  Venue is proper in the United States District Court for the District
    > of Arizona pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1391(b) because a substantial
    > part of the events or omissions giving rise to the claim occurred
    > in this District, specifically related to the sale and attempted
    > transfer of title of the vehicle.

**III. PARTIES**

6.  Plaintiff John Smith is an individual residing in \[City, Arizona\].

7.  Defendant SuperDoo Inc. is a corporation doing business in the State
    > of Arizona, including engaging in the sale of motor vehicles.

**IV. FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS**

8.  On or about \[Date of Purchase\], Plaintiff John Smith purchased a
    > \[Year, Make, Model of Car\] vehicle (the \"Vehicle\") from
    > Defendant SuperDoo Inc. at its location in \[City, Arizona\].

9.  Plaintiff paid Defendant SuperDoo Inc. the full purchase price for
    > the Vehicle, totaling approximately \$\[Purchase Price\].

10. At the time of sale, Defendant SuperDoo Inc. represented that it
    > would provide Plaintiff with a valid and transferable title for
    > the Vehicle within a reasonable time.

11. More than six months have passed since the date of purchase.

12. Despite repeated requests by Plaintiff, Defendant SuperDoo Inc. has
    > failed or refused to provide Plaintiff with a valid and
    > transferable title for the Vehicle.

13. Plaintiff is unable to register the Vehicle in his name or legally
    > transfer ownership of the Vehicle without a valid title.

14. As a direct result of Defendant\'s failure to provide the title, the
    > Vehicle\'s value has been diminished, Plaintiff has incurred
    > storage costs, and Plaintiff is unable to sell or otherwise
    > dispose of the Vehicle.

15. Defendant\'s failure to provide a valid title constitutes a breach
    > of the purchase agreement and/or a violation of applicable laws
    > regarding vehicle sales and title transfers.

**V. CLAIMS FOR RELIEF**

**COUNT I: BREACH OF CONTRACT**

16. Plaintiff incorporates by reference the allegations contained in
    > paragraphs 1 through 15 above.

17. Plaintiff and Defendant entered into a valid and binding contract
    > for the purchase and sale of the Vehicle.

18. Plaintiff performed all conditions precedent required by the
    > contract.

19. Defendant breached the contract by failing to provide a valid and
    > transferable title for the Vehicle within a reasonable time as
    > agreed.

20. As a direct and proximate result of Defendant\'s breach, Plaintiff
    > has suffered damages, including but not limited to, the diminished
    > value of the Vehicle, storage costs, and loss of use of the
    > Vehicle, in an amount to be proven at trial.

**COUNT II: \[Potential additional claim, e.g., Violation of State
Consumer Protection Laws - *This would require research into specific

21. Plaintiff incorporates by reference the allegations contained in
    > paragraphs 1 through 20 above.

22. \[Allegations detailing how Defendant\'s actions violated specific
    > provisions of relevant Arizona statutes concerning vehicle sales

23. As a direct and proximate result of Defendant\'s violations,
    > Plaintiff has suffered damages as described above and is entitled
    > to relief under the applicable statute, which may include treble
    > damages and attorneys\' fees.

**VI. PRAYER FOR RELIEF**

WHEREFORE, Plaintiff John Smith prays for judgment against Defendant
SuperDoo Inc. and requests that this Honorable Court grant the following
relief:

a\) Award Plaintiff compensatory damages in an amount to be proven at
trial;

b\) Award Plaintiff any additional damages permitted by law, including
potentially treble damages under applicable statutes;

c\) Award Plaintiff his reasonable attorneys\' fees and costs incurred
in this action;

d\) Order Defendant to immediately provide a valid and transferable
title for the Vehicle to Plaintiff;

e\) Grant Plaintiff such other and further relief as the Court deems
just and proper.

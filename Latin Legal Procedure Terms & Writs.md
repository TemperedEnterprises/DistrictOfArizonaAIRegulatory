# **Latin Legal Procedure Terms & Writs**

These terms relate to the process, actions, and formal documents used
within the court system.

-   **Affidavit:** \"He has sworn.\" A written statement confirmed by
    > oath or affirmation, for use as evidence in court.

-   **Amicus Curiae:** \"Friend of the court.\" An impartial adviser,
    > often a person or group not a party to a case, who assists the
    > court by offering information, expertise, or insight.

-   **Certiorari:** \"To be informed.\" A writ (an order from a higher
    > court) seeking judicial review of a decision by a lower court or
    > government agency. The Supreme Court uses *certiorari* to decide
    > which cases it will hear.

-   **De Bonis Non Administravis:** \"Of goods not administered.\"
    > Refers to remaining assets in an estate where the previous
    > administrator failed to complete the administration.

-   **De Novo:** \"Anew.\" Starting over, often referring to a new trial
    > or hearing.

-   **Ex Parte:** \"From one party.\" A legal proceeding brought by one
    > person in the absence of and without representation of other
    > parties.

-   **Habeas Corpus:** \"You shall have the body.\" A writ requiring a
    > person under arrest to be brought before a judge or into court,
    > especially to secure the person\'s release unless lawful grounds
    > are shown for their detention.

-   **In Camera:** \"In chambers.\" A hearing held in private, typically
    > in a judge\'s office, rather than in the open courtroom.

-   **In Limine:** \"At the threshold.\" A motion discussed at the start
    > of a trial, often to exclude evidence.

-   **In Re:** \"In the matter of.\" Used in case titles involving a
    > specific thing or status rather than opposing parties (e.g.,
    > probate or bankruptcy).

-   **Lis Pendens:** \"Suit pending.\" A pending legal action, often
    > used to give notice that property is subject to litigation.

-   **Locus Standi:** \"Place of standing.\" The right or capacity of a
    > party to bring an action or appear in court.

-   **Mandamus:** \"We command.\" A judicial writ ordering a person or
    > body to perform a public duty.

-   **Nolo Contendere:** \"I do not wish to contend.\" A plea in
    > criminal court where the defendant does not admit guilt but also
    > does not contest the charges, resulting in a conviction.

-   **Pendente Lite:** \"During litigation.\" Refers to court orders or
    > actions taken while a lawsuit is pending.

-   **Per Curiam:** \"By the court.\" A judgment delivered by the court
    > as a whole, without identifying a specific author.

-   **Sub Judice:** \"Under judgment.\" A case currently being
    > considered by a court.

-   **Subpoena:** \"Under penalty.\" A writ ordering a person to attend
    > a court or legislative hearing.

-   **Voir Dire:** (Technically Old French, but commonly used) \"To
    > speak the truth.\" The process of questioning prospective jurors
    > or witnesses to determine their suitability or competence.

# **Latin Legal Remedies**

Terms related to the legal relief or compensation granted by a court to
an injured party.

-   **Quantum Meruit:** \"As much as he/she deserved.\" A remedy in
    > restitution allowing recovery for the reasonable value of services
    > rendered when there was no explicit contract but a benefit was
    > conferred and accepted under circumstances where payment was
    > expected.

-   **Quantum Valebant:** \"As much as they were worth.\" Similar to
    > quantum meruit, but specifically for the reasonable value of goods
    > provided.

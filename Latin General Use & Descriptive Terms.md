# **Latin General Use & Descriptive Terms**

These terms are used more broadly as modifiers, connectors, or general
descriptions within legal writing and discourse.

-   **Ad Hoc:** \"For this \[purpose\].\" Created or done for a
    > particular purpose as needed.

-   **Ad Infinitum:** \"To infinity.\" Continuing without limit.

-   **Ad Valorem:** \"According to value.\" A tax or duty based on the
    > assessed value of an item.

-   **Dictum (Plural: Dicta):** \"Something said by the way.\" A remark
    > or observation made by a judge that is not necessary to the
    > decision of the case and therefore not binding precedent (*Obiter
    > Dictum*).

-   **Et Al. (Et Alii):** \"And others.\" Used in case names or lists to
    > refer to other parties not explicitly named.

-   **Et Cetera (Etc.):** \"And other things.\"

-   **Id Est (i.e.):** \"That is.\" Used to clarify or rephrase a
    > preceding statement.

-   **Exempli Gratia (e.g.):** \"For the sake of example.\" Used to
    > introduce an example.

-   **In Situ:** \"In the original place.\"

-   **In Toto:** \"In total\" or \"entirely.\"

-   **Inter Alia:** \"Among other things.\" Used to indicate that the
    > item mentioned is one of several items in a list or group.

-   **Ipso Facto:** \"By the fact itself.\" By the very nature of the
    > deed.

-   **Modus Operandi (M.O.):** \"Method of operating.\" A person\'s
    > particular way of doing things, often used in criminal
    > investigations.

-   **Modus Vivendi:** \"Way of living.\" A practical compromise or
    > arrangement allowing conflicting parties to coexist.

-   **Mutatis Mutandis:** \"The necessary changes having been made.\"
    > Used when comparing two situations, implying that necessary
    > alterations have been considered.

-   **Obiter Dictum:** \"Something said by the way.\" (See Dictum)

-   **Pari Passu:** \"On equal footing.\" Proportionately or equally.

-   **Per Capita:** \"By head.\" Distribution of an estate equally among
    > all beneficiaries.

-   **Per Stirpes:** \"By roots.\" Distribution of an estate where
    > shares pass to the descendants of deceased beneficiaries.

-   **Pro Rata:** \"In proportion.\" Proportionally calculated.

-   **Pro Tempore (Pro Tem):** \"For the time being.\" Temporarily.

-   **Ratio Decidendi:** \"The reason for deciding.\" The essential
    > legal principle that forms the basis of a court\'s decision; this
    > is the binding part of a judgment.

-   **Sine Die:** \"Without a day.\" Adjourning indefinitely.

-   **Sine Qua Non:** \"Without which not.\" An essential condition;
    > something indispensable.

-   **Status Quo:** \"The existing state of affairs.\"

-   **Vice Versa:** \"The other way around.\"
